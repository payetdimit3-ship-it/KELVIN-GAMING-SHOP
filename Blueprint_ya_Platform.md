## 🎯 4. COMPONENT KUU ZA COMMUNITY & YOUTH HUB

### 4.1 💼 Youth Opportunities (Kazi na Fursa)
Mfumo: **Opportunity → Apply → Review → Accepted → Task → Payment/Reward**
- Gaming jobs & Tournament referees
- Web development, UI/UX & Graphic design
- Video editing, Content creation & Social media
- Customer support & Game testing
- Marketing/affiliate opportunities & Digital-product sellers

### 4.2 🎓 Gaming & Technology Academy
Mfumo: **Learn → Course → Lesson → Quiz → Certificate** (Free na Premium)
- Mada: HTML/CSS, JavaScript, React, UI/UX, Graphic Design, Video Editing, Game Development, AI, Cybersecurity basics, Digital Marketing, Entrepreneurship
- **AI Mentor**: mwalimu binafsi anayeongoza masomo hatua kwa hatua

### 4.3 🤝 Community Help Center
- Elimu ya afya na matumizi salama ya mtandao
- Elimu ya dawa za kulevya na Mental Health (kwa mujibu wa miongozo ya Wizara ya Afya)
- Financial & Digital literacy, Youth guidance
- Taarifa za dharura na links za huduma rasmi za serikali/taasisi

### 4.4 🩺 AI Health Assistant (Msaidizi wa Afya)
- **Mfumo wa Mazungumzo** (Chat/Voice): triage kwa maswali ya msingi yanayoendelea (siyo majibu ya sentensi moja)
- **Ngazi za Urgency:**
  - 🟢 Low concern — ushauri wa jumla
  - 🟡 Needs attention — mshauri aonane na mtaalamu
  - 🔴 Urgent/Emergency — elekeza dharura mara moja
- **Mipaka & Safety Layer:** haijifanyi daktari, haitoi prescription/diagnosis ya uhakika. Inafuata miongozo ya WHO (Safety, Privacy, Human Oversight)

### 4.5 ❤️ Addiction Recovery Support AI
- Msaada bila kumhukumu mtumiaji
- Emotional support, Referral kwa vituo vya rehab/afya
- Recovery planning (goals, reminders, progress tracking)
- **Crisis detection** — dalili za kujidhuru/overdose → msaada wa dharura papo hapo

### 4.6 👨‍⚕️ Professional Health Network
- Mtandao wa wataalamu waliothibitishwa (Doctors, Nurses, Counselors)
- Mtumiaji anaunganishwa na mtaalamu halisi baada ya AI triage ya awali

### 4.7 🏆 Youth Talent Marketplace & Creator Program
- **Marketplace:** vijana wanaweka portfolio (mf. designers) na kuajiriwa na watumiaji wengine
- **Creator Program:** wanauza game guides, graphics, sound effects, templates, coding services — platform inachukua commission ndogo

### 4.8 🏥 Community Support Fund
- Sehemu ya mapato ya platform inasaidia vijana wasiojiweza na miradi ya jamii
- **Dashboard ya Uwazi:** kiasi kilichokusanywa, kilichogawiwa, na miradi iliyofaidika

## ⚠️ 9. Vipengele Vilivyoachwa au Kubadilishwa (Sababu za Kisheria/Usalama)

| Wazo la Awali | Tatizo | Mbadala Uliopendekezwa |
|---|---|---|

| Automated Account Trading (auto password-change) | Automation ya kuingia akaunti za watu bila ruhusa | Escrow ya kawaida — mnunuzi/muuzaji wanahamishana credentials wenyewe, Admin anathibitisha |
| PSP In-Browser Emulator + ROM/ISO za games | Piracy ikiwa hauna haki za maudhui | Emulator tech (EmulatorJS) ni sawa; maudhui lazima yawe yako halali |

| "Stealth AI" inayojifanya binadamu | Udanganyifu kwa mteja | AI inajitambulisha wazi kama AI (tayari imejengwa hivyo) |
| "Full Knowledge Freedom" bila mipaka | Hatari kwa Health AI hasa | Mipaka ya Health AI (WHO guidelines) inabaki |


- **Frontend:** HTML/CSS/JS ya kawaida (module kwa module)
- **Database:** Supabase
- **Hosting:** Render
- **AI Layer:** AI Team (9 agents) + AI Boss Assistant, na **Health AI iliyotengwa (isolated)** kwenye Safety Layer yake
- **Payments:** **AzamPay** (gateway moja inayounganisha M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel Money, HaloPesa, AzamPesa, na Bank CRDB/NMB) — kupitia Supabase Edge Functions (`azampay-checkout` + `azampay-callback`), angalia `AzamPay_Integration.md`
- **Security:** Permissions + audit logs

**Mpango wa kazi:** kuendelea module kwa module (kama ilivyokwisha anza), kisha kuunganisha Supabase schema ili data zote (bidhaa, orders, tournaments, users, youth opportunities) ziache kuwa placeholder.
